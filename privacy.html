Privacy Policy

HealthConnect Nigeria is a healthcare discovery and appointment-request project. This page explains the intended privacy approach for the prototype.

Launch checklist: This draft must be reviewed and completed by the actual operator before public launch. Replace all bracketed details, confirm the real data flows, and obtain Nigerian privacy-law advice where appropriate.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features enabled, information may include account name, email address, phone number, role, provider profile details, appointment request details, and messages you choose to submit. Do not submit emergency details or unnecessary medical information through this prototype.

Why information is used

Information should only be used to provide account access, display provider listings, route appointment requests, protect the service, and respond to support or deletion requests. The live operator must document any additional purpose before collecting data for it.

Storage and sharing

This project currently includes prototype flows that may store some information in the browser. It is not approved for real patient records. Before launch, the operator must document server/database storage, access controls, processors, hosting locations, retention periods, and any sharing with healthcare providers.

Security and retention

Reasonable safeguards should include access controls, secure transport, protected backups, least-privilege access, audit logging, and a tested incident-response plan. No website can promise absolute security. Keep personal information only as long as necessary for a defined purpose or legal obligation.

Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information where applicable. Use the data request page. The operator should verify your identity safely before acting.

Children and sensitive information

The operator must define age-related safeguards and lawful handling before accepting information about children. Do not submit sensitive health information until the live service has been reviewed and approved for that use.
