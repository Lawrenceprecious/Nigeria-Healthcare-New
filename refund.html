Refund Policy

HealthConnect Nigeria currently presents itself as a discovery and appointment-request prototype and does not show a paid checkout. Do not accept payments until pricing, payment handling, cancellations, and refund terms are implemented and reviewed.

Current status

No paid service or payment checkout is represented as active in this prototype. Do not send money to anyone claiming to collect a HealthConnect fee unless the official operator and payment process have been independently verified.

If paid services are introduced

The operator must publish clear prices, what the fee covers, cancellation rules, refund eligibility, processing timelines, and a monitored support contact before taking payment. Provider consultation fees may be governed by the provider's own disclosed policy.
